Overlays, themes & accessibility+

Choose Blue, Green or Monochrome application themes in light and dark variants, then customize in-game overlay palettes independently. The visual editor supports dragging panels, anchors, per-panel scale and opacity, typography and visibility controls. Keyboard and controller shortcuts provide quick access during play.

The interface includes English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Português (Brasil), Русский and 简体中文. Searchable Guides organize instructions by subject; settings search and horizontal table scrolling help with larger workspaces.

Reports, bookmarks & saved work+

Keep current and historical mining reports, notes, screenshots and manual refinery estimates. Export offline HTML reports for printing or PDF, import CSV history, and make ZIP mining backups. Bookmarks support categories, notes, screenshot attachments and JSON import/export; Surface Mining adds favorites and deposit details.

Optional Google Drive sync carries supported settings and saved work between machines. Machine-dependent overlay positions, monitor choices and capture calibration stay tied to their originating computer. Local backup and restore work without a Google account.

Diagnostics, migration & platform support+

Inspect journal sources, individual events, parsed live state and application logs. Historical journals can rebuild state; exported diagnostic replays help reproduce issues. An importer can bring in an existing SrvSurvey profile while preserving the source.

Windows x64 and Linux x64 packages include their runtime. Linux overlays support X11 and XWayland; compositor behavior varies, and pure native Wayland does not provide full overlay functionality. Where supported, screen capture uses the desktop portal and PipeWire, and keyboard input can use the Global Shortcuts portal.

OPTIONAL GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP & SYNC

Continue where
you left off.

Link Google Drive in Settings → Data & migration. Sync supported settings, searches, routes, bookmarks and surveys between computers, or restore a saved backup.

  • Startup sync and backup on orderly shutdown
  • Manual Backup now / Sync now and conflict choices
  • Machine-specific positions and capture settings
  • Cloud history, downloads and confirmed deletion

Uses Drive’s private app-data folder, with permission limited to that folder. Credentials, raw journals and screenshot files are excluded. Google sign-in is optional.

Frontier account linking

Optional OAuth linking adds commander, ship, carrier, market and Community Goal information to your local dashboard. SrvSurvey does not receive your Frontier password.

Reference & community services

Canonn, Spansh, EDSM, Inara and other catalogs provide public discovery, route, system and market information. Enabled Raven Colonial workflows synchronize project and supply information.

Separate sharing controls

EDDN, Inara, direct EDSM, VoxStellar and supported discovery uploads have independent opt-in requirements. Commander identifiers and gameplay data may be sent when you enable the relevant service.

Clear about data

Local journals power the app. Daily activity reporting sends a random installation ID, app version and operating system to count active installations; it does not send your commander name, journal or Google account.
